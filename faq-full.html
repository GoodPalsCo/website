The basics

What is Good Pals?

Good Pals is people teaming up, with artists making, pals buying, and pets benefiting.

You choose a piece of custom pet art, chip in what feels right, and that money goes toward a pet who needs help with their vet bills.

Who counts as a pal?

Everyone! Artists, pet parents, art lovers, people hunting for a one-of-a-kind gift, and anyone who just likes the idea. You don't need an art degree, and you don't even need a pet.

How does it work?
  1. Pick an artist and a style you love.
  2. Send us a photo of your pet (or someone else's, if it's a gift).
  3. Choose your amount on the slider, anywhere from $20 to $100.
  4. The artist creates your piece and it's delivered to you.
  5. Your money goes toward a pet's care. Everyone wins, especially the pet.
Where does my money go?

Straight toward the pet's care. A small percentage keeps the site running, and we'll always be upfront about the split.

Is the money paid directly to the vet clinic or to the pet parent? Add the platform percentage once it's decided (0% for the Nala pilot).

Can I give it as a gift?

Absolutely. Just send us a photo of their pet, and you can add them to the Pal Wall too. It's the perfect gift for the person who has everything (except a portrait of their dog).

What kind of photo should I send?

The clearer the better: soft natural light, eyes in focus, and your pet's face filling most of the frame. A couple of extra photos help the artist get their markings and personality just right. Phone pics are perfect, no photoshoot needed.

Pets & causes

Which pets do you help?

Our very first pal is Nala, a gentle 6-year-old cat and neighbourhood favourite who's going through treatment right now. As we grow, we'll be adding more pets and rescues.

Optional: link the "Meet Nala" Instagram post here once it's up.
Is Good Pals pet insurance?

Nope, and it's not meant to replace it. Good Pals is for the sudden, rare things that aren't covered, even when a pet is insured.

Will it cover the whole vet bill?

The goal is to make a big dent in it, so pet parents aren't stuck choosing between debt, rehoming or worse.

How do you choose which pets to help?

Every cause goes through a vetting process before it's added, so pals always know who they're helping.

For artists

I'm an artist. How do I join?

We'd love to create with you! Sign up and pick "Artist," or email hello@goodpals.ca with a link to your work.

What do artists get out of it?

To start, artists give their time to create pieces for the cause. In return, you get credit on every piece, tags and shout-outs on our channels, and your work in front of a whole community of people who love pets and art.

Down the road, there'll be a marketplace where you can sell your own work, with a percentage going straight to the cause.
